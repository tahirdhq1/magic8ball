About Magic 8 Ball Online

🔮 Digital Fortune Teller & Decision Maker

Magic 8 Ball Online is a modern, interactive web application bringing the world's most beloved fortune-telling toy into the digital age with rich animations, sound effects, and privacy-first local storage.

✨ The Classic 20 Answers

Faithful to the original 1950s oracle created by Albert C. Carter and Abe Bookman, our ball contains the classic 20 predictions: 10 affirmative, 5 non-committal, and 5 negative outcomes.

⚡ Instant Pseudo-Random Oracle

Our algorithm ensures unbiased random selection across all outcomes every time you ask a question or shake the mystical ball.

Your questions, history, and favorite answers stay exclusively inside your browser's local storage. We never collect or sell your personal queries.
